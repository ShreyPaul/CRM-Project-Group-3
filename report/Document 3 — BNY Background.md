## Company profile
The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation (BNY) is a global financial services company headquartered in New York. It serves institutional clients across the investment life cycle through three segments: **Securities Services; Market and Wealth Services; Investment and Wealth Management**. Activities include custody and asset servicing, payments, clearing and collateral services, and investment management (BNY, 2025).

## Relevance to insider risk and monitoring
- BNY handles sensitive client information, financial transactions, and systems requiring reliable operation. Its annual report lists information theft, loss, unauthorised disclosure, and alteration among its cybersecurity risks — making **data exfiltration** and **privileged-user misuse** the relevant scenarios (BNY, 2025).
- Electronic communications create recordkeeping obligations. In August 2024, the SEC imposed a joint-and-several **$40 million penalty** on BNY Mellon Securities Corporation and Pershing LLC for recordkeeping violations involving off-channel business communications. This concerns recordkeeping, not a data-theft incident, but shows the financial relevance of communications governance (SEC, 2024).

## Use in this project
BNY supplies the organisational context and the workforce scale (48,100) for the turnover calculation. Public disclosures establish context only; attack rates, control effectiveness, and employee responses are separate simulation assumptions (Document 4). The three regimes are analytical scenarios, not descriptions of BNY's actual monitoring.
